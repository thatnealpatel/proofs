# Rank-47 Conjectures

This file retains only conjectures with direct relevance to the rank-47 classification problem or to the coordinated multi-term deformation program. Exact results, implementation facts, policy experiments, non-invariant statements, and conjectures supported only by one known orbit have been removed.

## Universal rank-47 infinitesimal rigidity

Every full-span GF(2) rank-47 decomposition of 4x4 matrix multiplication has Brent-Jacobian kernel equal to the tangent space generated by term rescalings and the infinitesimal sandwich action. Equivalently, both spaces have dimension 139 and the first-order normal tangent quotient vanishes.

**Test:** For every newly discovered rank-47 class, construct the trivial tangent generators explicitly and compare their span with the exact Jacobian kernel. Any nontrivial quotient tangent refutes the conjecture.

## Existence of a fourth rank-47 orbit

There exists a full-span GF(2) rank-47 orbit outside the established AlphaTensor, Kauers--Moosbauer, and c680 classes.

**Test:** Reject the three known orientation-canonical invariants during independent generation, then certify each survivor with the exact sandwich-equivalence solver.

## Intrinsic restriction ladder at rank 47

Every full-span GF(2) rank-47 decomposition of 4x4 matrix multiplication admits:

1. a compatible rank-2 idempotent restriction leaving exactly seven nonzero summands; and
2. a compatible rank-3 idempotent restriction leaving at most 24 nonzero summands.

The restrictions are intrinsic split-subspace restrictions, not coordinate minors.

**Test:** For every new class, enumerate all compatible rank-2 and rank-3 idempotent triples. A rank-2 minimum above 7 or a rank-3 minimum above 24 refutes the corresponding clause.

## Universal native irredundancy at rank 47

Every full-span GF(2) rank-47 decomposition has 47 distinct projective factors in each leg and full rank 47 in all three complementary Khatri--Rao families.

**Test:** Any new class with a repeated projective factor or a deficient complementary family refutes the conjecture and supplies an exposed ordinary-flip or linear-reduction mechanism.

## Rank-48 transition barrier between known classes

No pair among AlphaTensor, Kauers--Moosbauer, and c680 is connected by a valid composite path whose maximum rank is 48, with term permutations, tensor orientations, and sandwich equivalences treated as zero-cost.

**Test:** One independently verified rank-ceiling-48 path refutes the conjecture. A proof requires exhaustive equivalence-reduced enumeration with persisted parent and move certificates; failed random search is not evidence.
