CLAIM
  A005432(n) = number of distinct subgroups of S_n
  (conjugates counted separately). Known upper bound
  matches c^(n^2 (1 + o(1))) with c = 2^(1/16) = the
  lower-bound constant; Pyber conjectures the lower
  bound is the truth, i.e.
    log2 a(n) = (1/16) n^2 (1 + o(1)).

LEAN
  Subgroup (Equiv.Perm (Fin n)) is Fintype-able:
  subgroups embed in Finset G with a decidable
  closure predicate (this corrects an ABSENT-rated
  audit — the instance may be missing upstream but is
  routine to provide). Asymptotic statement via
  Real.log and IsBigO/IsLittleO atTop. M.

ROUTE
  Open mathematics (elementary-abelian 2-subgroup
  counting gives the lower bound — that HALF is a
  real but approachable formalization: count
  subgroups inside an elementary abelian 2-subgroup
  of rank ~n/2... still substantial; grade L).

EVIDENCE
  Asymptotic bounds as cited in-entry.
