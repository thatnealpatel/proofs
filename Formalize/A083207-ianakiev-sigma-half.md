# A083207 — Ianakiev sigma-half closure

- **Mathematical status:** open conjecture (Ivan N. Ianakiev, OEIS A083207, 2017-04-03).
- **Work status:** hard-blocked; no proof route is known.
- **Remaining target:** among every four consecutive terms in the increasing enumeration of Zumkeller numbers, prove that some `k` has `sigma(k)/2` Zumkeller.
- **Proved prerequisites:** `Proofs/Enumerative/ZumkellerSigmaHalf.lean` defines the exact proposition `IanakievSigmaHalf`, proves the Zumkeller set infinite and pins its first terms; it also proves the published Bhaskara Rao–Peng characterization for practical numbers. The proposition definition is not a proof.
- **Next obligation:** obtain a distribution theorem for Zumkeller numbers and their half-sigma values, beyond the reported finite verification of the first `10^5` terms.
