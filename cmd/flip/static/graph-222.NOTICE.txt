M2 orbit flip-graph data attribution

The topology and representative identifiers in graph-222.json are derived from
the `222flipgraph_rank8` dataset in:

  Flip Graphs for Matrix Multiplication
  Jakob Moosbauer and Michael Poole
  https://github.com/jakobmoosbauer/flips
  revision e31a0a0f0d2577cee5da047ca7dcae0c61992e40
  paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.01175

later. Its license text is reproduced in graph-222.LICENSE.txt. The graph JSON
was independently generated from the published `.exp` representatives and
`edges.txt`; it does not copy program source or figure geometry. The generated
layout and highlighted deterministic shortest path are additions in this
project.
